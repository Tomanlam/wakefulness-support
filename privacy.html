Wakefulness Privacy Policy

Wakefulness is a classroom tool for teachers. The Sample Class contains fictional student records and is available without signing in.

ManageBac

If you connect a school account, Wakefulness displays the school's ManageBac sign-in page in Apple's embedded browser. ManageBac and its sign-in provider handle the credentials you enter. Wakefulness uses the signed-in session to retrieve the teacher account identifier, class rosters, available student photos, and attendance information. Wakefulness sends reviewed attendance changes to the school's ManageBac service when you choose to sync. The school's policies and ManageBac's privacy terms apply to the information they process.

On-device classroom records

Wakefulness stores imported class information, local attendance drafts, observations, and associated photos in the app's storage on your device. If you use the optional Napdex camera, the photo stays in Wakefulness's storage and is not added to your photo library or sent to ManageBac. Wakefulness excludes its classroom storage from device backups. The app does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Retention and removal

Local classroom records remain in app storage until removed. If you remove a Napdex photo or delete the observation it belongs to, Wakefulness removes the associated local photo. Signing out clears the ManageBac web session. To remove the app's remaining local records from the device, delete the app rather than offloading it. Attendance already synced to ManageBac remains subject to the school's retention and deletion policies.

Privacy contact

Use the published Wakefulness support contact for questions about the app's handling of data or requests concerning information you sent to support. For school records held in ManageBac, contact your school's administrator.

See the ManageBac Global Privacy Notice for more information about its service.
