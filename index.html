Wakefulness Support

Wakefulness helps teachers take attendance, record classroom observations, and review lesson trends on iPhone and iPad.

Try the Sample Class on the first launch. It uses fictional student records and requires no account.

For live attendance, use an authorized Han Academy ManageBac teacher account. In Attendance, choose a period, stage the marks, and check them in Review & Sync before sending anything to ManageBac. Contact your school’s ManageBac administrator for account access or register permissions.

When reporting an issue to the Wakefulness support contact, include your app version, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. Leave out student names, photos, school records, and passwords.

Email toman.lam@hanacademy.edu.hk.
